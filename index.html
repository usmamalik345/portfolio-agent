AI agent · engineering case study

Agent Receptionist

Tool-calling LLM agent (Groq, gpt-oss-120b) for appointment-based businesses. RAG with cited sources, policy pre-routing for emergencies, hours and handoff at zero tokens, a per-sentence guard, a deterministic booking fallback, and streaming SSE replies. Measured: deterministic eval 44/44, live Groq eval 43/44 (one eval-regex miss), live 20-question test 19/20, about $0.0002 per question in one live check. Demos use fictional businesses; source is private, available on request.

  • before model
    Policy pre-router. Rules decide emergencies, hours and handoff.
  • after model
    Per-sentence guard. Entity allowlist, hours check, required-fact check.
  • security
    Secrets and raw provider errors stay server-side.
